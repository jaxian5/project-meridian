Studio

A small studio for load-bearing software.

Skibitech LLC was founded in Mariveles in 2026 by engineers and designers who had spent enough years in larger rooms. We wanted the people who discover a problem to be the people who ship the system — and to stay for the years after launch, when the real edge cases arrive.

The name

Ski line, then tech. Commit early. Hold it.

On a mountain, the cleanest descent is not the one with the most turns. It is the one you choose from the ridge and then hold — through the ice, the wind, the moment you would rather decorate. We named the studio for that. Skibitech is not a ski company. It is a reminder about taste under load.

We work with operators whose software has become the business: regional carriers, water authorities, clinics, collectives. The industries change. The requirement does not. The system has to hold, in the hands of the people who run the day.

How we take the work

Principles

    Go to the work

    If we have not stood where the software will be used, we are not ready to draw it.

    One team, one thread

    The people who discover the problem are the people who ship the system. Handoffs are where judgment dies.

    Fewer accounts

    We take on a small number of partnerships at a time. Depth is the product. A crowded roster is a different business.

    Hold the line

    The cleanest line down a mountain is the one you commit to early and hold. Scope, architecture, and taste work the same way.
