How we work

Short discovery. A working slice. Then the years that matter.

    01

    Discover

    We go to where the work happens. Floors, yards, clinics, sheds. Two weeks of attention before a single screen. The brief is written from what we saw, not what was requested.

    02

    Shape

    A working slice, not a deck. We name the smallest thing that would change the week, design it in the stack we will ship, and put it in front of the people who will use it.

    03

    Build

    The team that shaped the work builds it. Design, engineering, and the operator stay in one thread. Releases are small, observable, and reversible.

    04

    Steward

    We do not disappear at launch. The useful life of a system is the years after it ships. We stay on for the season when the real edge cases arrive.
